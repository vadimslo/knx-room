5.1 Pipeline by named counterparty and stage

Tier 1 Filed

Three enterprise counterparties, Exyn Technologies, ReadyMonitor and Veloce, are integrating their workloads ahead of mainnet, to run as subnets. Beyond the three enterprise counterparties, the pipeline consists of one counterparty-signed MOU, one executed marketing agreement, one advisory NDA, two counterparties at discussion stage, four independent validator operators and the subnet-builder cohort.
