2.8 Market making

No market maker is engaged. The Token Release Schedule of 19 October 2026 sets the liquidity / market-making pocket at 10,000,000 KNX (1.00% of supply), released at TGE (target Q1 2027, model working date 5 February 2027) as a returnable market-maker loan to a market maker to be appointed before listing, with terms to be published; no tokens have been lent and no option has been written. Two proposals were received in 2026 and neither was executed; Strata is engaged for launch advisory under a mutual NDA.

Strata

A mutual non-disclosure agreement between Cryptide Limited (Strata) and the Konnex contracting entity took effect on 21 June 2026: three-year term, terminable on 30 days' notice, confidentiality surviving two years, Marshall Islands law. The NDA creates no obligation to proceed with any transaction. Strata's role is launch advisory; no treasury-management, market-making or listing-fee agreement has been executed with Strata.
