Section 7 · Legal and regulatory

7.2 Token classification by jurisdiction for the markets we will operate in

Tier 2 TOKEN Filed, partial

In preparation. Classification memo covering the US, the EU (MiCA whitepaper), the UK and the issuing jurisdictions.

KNX has been assessed as unlikely to be a security in Singapore (Chung Ting Fai & Co, 3 February 2026) and in Korea (Lee & Ko, 24 July 2026). In the European Union the company's stated position is that KNX is not an asset-referenced token or an e-money token under MiCA, with a MiCA-compliant whitepaper in preparation. No registration statement has been filed anywhere. The company's own primary distributions exclude the United States and US persons, Singapore, mainland China and sanctioned or FATF call-for-action jurisdictions; secondary trading and use of the network carry no geographic restriction imposed by the project, and protocol services are not offered in Korea.

Classification by jurisdiction

JurisdictionPosition
SingaporeUnlikely a security token or other capital markets product under the Securities and Futures Act; unlikely a digital payment token and not e-money under the Payment Services Act. Given on the basis that the company has no operations or customers in Singapore and does not intend to offer KNX there; Singapore is accordingly excluded from the company's primary distributions.
KoreaLow likelihood that KNX meets the general definition of a security or any of the six sub-types under the Financial Investment Services and Capital Markets Act, including the investment-contract security. Escrow, settlement, bridge withdrawals and the fiat on-ramp are not offered to users in Korea; Korean residents and Korean-incorporated entities are excluded in the terms of service and at onboarding, with IP geo-blocking as a secondary control; no Korean-language interface, KRW support, Korean marketing or app-store distribution; the company conducts no virtual-asset, electronic-financial or cross-border settlement business in or into Korea.
European UnionKNX is not backed by or redeemable for any fiat or other asset, uses no collateralisation and is not asset-referenced; it is not an asset-referenced token (ART) or an e-money token (EMT) under MiCA and the company is not registered as an ART or EMT issuer. A MiCA-compliant whitepaper is in preparation.
United StatesNo registered public offering has been conducted and no registration statement has been filed with any securities regulator. The asset was not offered to US investors in the completed rounds. The United States and US persons are excluded from the project's own primary distributions (token sales and the community airdrop claim portal). The project imposes no restriction on secondary trading; availability on any exchange is a matter for that exchange's own licensing.
Issuing jurisdictions (British Virgin Islands, Panama)The token issuer is Dalithium Inc (BVI); its parent is Dalithium Foundation (Panama). Neither opinion on file addresses these laws.

Characteristics relied on

  • KNX does not evidence indebtedness, is not marketed as debt, confers no right to interest, dividend or dividend equivalent at a stated maturity, and is not tied to a pool of assets that would let counterparties hedge or speculate on an underlying.
  • KNX does not represent ownership, equity, voting rights or a share of profits in any legal entity; planned governance rights relate to the protocol, not to any company.
  • Protocol fees are not distributed to KNX holders; KNX carries no dividend, revenue share, profit participation or redemption right; treasury market activity is discretionary and is not a commitment to purchase KNX or support its price (listing-review answer of 12 August 2026, point 3).
  • Token utilities at mainnet: validator staking with slashing, network and protocol fees, AI-miner collateral, on-chain governance, executor bonding and a buyback flywheel fed by a fee on stablecoin settlement; mainnet is pre-launch.

Primary distributions: restricted jurisdictions

Restrictions apply at the level of the project's own primary distributions only: any token sale and the community airdrop claim portal. Excluded are (i) the United States and US persons; (ii) Singapore; (iii) jurisdictions and regions subject to comprehensive sanctions or an FATF call for action, at the date of the answer Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Myanmar and the Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine, together with Russia and Belarus, and any person subject to applicable OFAC, UN, EU or UK sanctions; (iv) mainland China, where the offering of digital tokens to residents is prohibited under local law; and (v) any other jurisdiction where participation would be unlawful. Geo-IP controls, sanctions screening and on-chain wallet screening are applied to the claim portal and distribution flows (item 7.3).

Secondary trading and network use

The project imposes no geographic restriction on secondary trading of KNX; availability on any venue is determined by that venue's own licensing and jurisdictional permissions. The Konnex network is permissionless with no protocol-level geo-blocking of network access, staking or token utility; the project's own hosted front-ends geo-block the restricted jurisdictions above, and optional KYC and AML toggles are available for enterprise and public-sector contracts. Protocol services (escrow, settlement, bridge withdrawals, on-ramp) are not offered in Korea at launch; KNX trading is independent of protocol service availability.
