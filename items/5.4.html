5.4 Testnet cohort analysis: actives net of airdrop farming, retention, work verified

Tier 1 In preparation

In preparation. Farming-adjusted cohort analysis with methodology: actives net of quest and airdrop farming, retention, verified work. The room shows raw counts only.

Testnet activity is read live from the public explorer at subnets.testnet.konnex.world/explorer: chain height, registered networks, validators, miners and validator publications per subnet. The figures below are refreshed from the explorer API each time the room is built and carry the time of the snapshot. Counts are as reported by the chain; a cohort analysis net of quest and airdrop participation is prepared separately.

What the explorer shows

  • Overview: head and finalized block, total networks, validators and miners across all subnets.
  • Subnets: the workload classes with registered participants (drone navigation, robot-arm VLA, SLAM 3D mapping), their UIDs, active UIDs and emission totals.
  • Validators and miners: hotkeys, stake, incentive and activity per subnet.
  • Publications: each validator's scoring publication with its evidence bundle and verifier mode.

Sources

  • Konnex testnet explorer, https://subnets.testnet.konnex.world/explorer, and its API endpoints (overview, subnets, validators, miners, blocks, activity), read at build time; snapshot time stated in the tables below.
