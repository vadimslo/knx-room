2.2 Vesting and lockup schedule by holder class, with wallet addresses

Tier 1 TOKEN Filed

Every KNX pocket in the schedule has a stated release schedule and, except the new round, a Base wallet. No investor, team or emissions tokens are unlocked at TGE; the 77,000,000 KNX (7.70%) float is marketing, liquidity, airdrop and the first treasury and foundation tranches. The full supply already sits in ten Gnosis Safes on Base, distributed in June and July 2026 on the July 2026 allocation set; re-allocation of those balances to the allocation amounts and creation of the new round wallet are pending.

Signers, thresholds and enforcement

The per-allocation multisigs are controlled by Vladimir Smelov (Chief Executive Officer and Chief Technology Officer) and Pablo Pérez Pazos (Chief Business Development Officer) as authorised signers. Signature thresholds are to be fixed before TGE. Vesting is to be administered by a third-party provider, not yet selected, with allocations custodied in the per-pocket Safes and released per the published schedule; the schedule "hardcoded in smart contract" row reads "No" for every pocket. Distribution at TGE is on-chain from the per-allocation wallets, with the community airdrop released through a distribution platform.
