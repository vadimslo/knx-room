6.2 Use of funds by workstream with milestones

Tier 1 Filed

The round is allocated across seven workstreams, three quarters of it to the attestation stack, field deployment and demand: the artefact and the customers that every later milestone depends on. The allocation below is the operating plan of September 2026, stated against a planned round of USD 33,000,000; the Token Release Schedule sizes the round at USD 32,000,000 and the split applies to the amount closed.

Runway

The plan targets a 36-month runway at a blended spend of about USD 900,000 a month, carrying the company to fee-generating settlement volume. At 30.00% of supply allocated to investors after the new round, this is the last token round before mainnet revenue.

Milestones the allocation is measured against

  • Attestation and field deployment: Proof-of-Physical-Work records from real hardware on three device classes, 10,000 attested tasks across three customer sites in one regulated vertical, with published dispute and false-positive rates (Phase 1, first half of 2027).
  • Demand: three to five paid pilots at USD 50,000 to USD 150,000 each, contracted, third-party and invoiced; one insurer or broker pricing against a Konnex record (Phase 1).
  • Protocol and listing: listing priced at the round, buy-and-burn of protocol revenue live from the first block, monthly scoreboard from month one (Phase 2, Q3 2027).
