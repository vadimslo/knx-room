2.4 Unlocks and sell pressure

The sell-pressure model puts each month's unlock from the schedule against reference prices and a projected 30-day spot volume. At the main close price of USD 0.16 the TGE float of 53,000,000 KNX (5.30%) is worth USD 8,480,000 (USD 10,600,000 at the USD 0.20 listing reference) and contains no investor tokens; the first investor unlock at T+13 adds 17,441,667 KNX (USD 2,790,667) in a single month, of which 8,125,000 KNX is the new round and 2,083,333 KNX the legacy holders. Volume is an input the model does not supply: the projected 30-day volume cell is blank and marked INPUT REQUIRED.

The T+13 unlock is 32.91% of the day-one float. At USD 0.16 the T+14 to T+24 run rate is USD 3,110,667 per month, about USD 37,300,000 a year; year two (T+13 to T+24) releases 231,300,000 KNX, USD 37,008,000 at USD 0.16 and USD 46,260,000 at USD 0.20. The monthly unlock steps down to 18,931,667 KNX from T+25 to T+30 (emissions at 2,890,000), 18,431,667 KNX from T+31 to T+36 (marketing complete), 6,539,833 KNX from T+37 (legacy, emissions, team) and 4,539,833 KNX from T+39 to T+48 (legacy and emissions). The ratio the model reports is unlock value divided by 30-day spot volume; as arithmetic, holding the T+13 unlock at 10% of turnover requires USD 27,906,667 of volume over the 30 days, about USD 930,000 a day, sustained from the first unlock. That is a threshold illustration, not a volume assumption; the volume itself is the blank input.

The largest line at T+13 is the new round, released at 8,125,000 KNX per month over 24 months and bought at USD 0.16 (main close) and USD 0.20 (top-up close). The legacy holders release 2,083,333 KNX per month, spread over 48 months.

Front-running of the calendar

Unlock calendars are public, and market research on unlock events finds that price impact typically begins about thirty days before the scheduled date as holders position ahead of it, with roughly nine in ten unlock events followed by short-term price declines and team unlocks the most damaging class. Applied to the schedule, the first calendar events are the KOLpad releases of 6,000,000 KNX at T+1 and T+2; the T+13 step begins to price in during T+12, and the T+14 team release, which is the class the research treats as most sensitive, sits one month behind it. The schedule mitigates this in three ways that are visible in the grid: no team, investor or advisor token unlocks at TGE or within the first year; the legacy investor release is spread over 48 months; and the emissions cap falls each year. What the schedule cannot do is supply volume, which is why the model leaves it as an input.
