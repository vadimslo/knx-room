2.5 Foundation governance, treasury policy, and DevCo / Foundation decision rights

Tier 1 TOKEN Filed

Dalithium Foundation, a Panama private-interest foundation, owns 100% of Dalithium Inc (BVI), the token issuer and development company, and holds the Foundation allocation of 40,000,000 KNX (4.00%). Protocol governance is designed as stake-weighted on-chain voting after mainnet; until then the development team holds runtime-upgrade authority, which is an effective veto. Day-to-day control of the Foundation, and through it of the issuer and of every token wallet, runs through three instruments executed on 27 May 2026: the Protector appointment, a General Power of Attorney and a nominee founder and council; the authorised signers on all ten Safes are Vladimir Smelov and Pablo Pérez Pazos, the latter also the Foundation's Protector and Attorney-in-Fact, with thresholds still to be fixed before TGE.

Treasury policy as stated to exchanges

  • Protocol fees are not distributed to KNX holders. KNX carries no dividend, revenue share, profit participation or redemption right.
  • The settlement fee is 50 bps (0.50%) on stablecoin settlement flows, with a separate bridge-withdrawal fee to be fixed at launch; the recipient is a single on-chain treasury contract whose address is published before activation.
  • Treasury market activity, including the buyback flywheel described in the whitepaper, is discretionary under a published policy and is not a commitment to purchase KNX or to support its price; no price or return representation is made.
  • Any change to the fee rate, recipient or use is disclosed in advance; upgrades are timelocked with public notice.

The Monetary Policy tab states separately that protocol revenue is directed to open-market buy-and-burn from TGE.
