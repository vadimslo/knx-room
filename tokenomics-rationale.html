Tokenomics rationale

Three design choices answer the first questions a lead investor asks about KNX supply: what the day-one float holds and what it cost, how large the month-13 unlock is, and how emissions are bounded. Every figure is from the Token Release Schedule of 19 October 2026.

1. The float needs a cost basis

At TGE, 53,000,000 KNX (5.30% of supply) is unlocked, worth USD 8,480,000 at the main close price of USD 0.16, which is 26.50% of the USD 32,000,000 round, and USD 10,600,000 at the USD 0.20 listing reference. The float is marketing obligations of 35,000,000 KNX (venue 1 25,000,000; venue 2 6,000,000; a decentralized-exchange pool on Base paired with USDC 4,000,000), a returnable market-maker loan of 10,000,000 KNX, the community airdrop of 5,000,000 KNX and the first KOLpad tranche of 3,000,000 KNX. No investor, team or advisor token is in it; every investor sits behind a twelve-month cliff, and Treasury and Foundation release nothing at TGE. By share of the float: marketing obligations 66.04%, market-maker loan 18.87%, airdrop 9.43%, KOLpad 5.66%.

The design answers the first question a lead asks in two ways. Nothing in the float was bought. The KOLpad allocations are KNX for the KOLs of the Mindo Solutions Inc. campaign and Mindo's commission, counted at a 40% discount to the TGE valuation (effective USD 0.12 at the USD 0.20 reference); the KOLs' contributions are returned to them before TGE, so these tokens carry no cash cost basis either. 3,000,000 KNX of them are liquid at TGE and all 15,000,000 KNX by T+2. Every line in the float has a stated purpose. Marketing, the largest single line at 66.04%, is capped at 35,000,000 KNX at TGE and itemized against listing and liquidity obligations, with the other 15,000,000 KNX of the pocket released at 500,000 KNX a month over T+1 to T+30. Liquidity is sized at 10,000,000 KNX (1.00% of supply) as a returnable market-maker loan with terms to be published. FDV at the reference is USD 200,000,000 and 94.70% of supply is locked at listing; the round's tokens begin to unlock at T+13.

2. The month-13 wall

Every investor cliff ends at T+13. In the schedule the T+13 unlock is 17,441,667 KNX, USD 2,790,667 at USD 0.16 and USD 3,488,333 at USD 0.20, or 32.91% of the day-one float: new round 8,125,000 (main close 7,291,667, top-up 833,333), emissions 3,400,000, legacy investors 2,083,333, treasury 1,666,667, Foundation 1,666,667 and marketing 500,000. The team release of 2,000,000 KNX per month begins at T+14, taking the monthly total to 19,441,667 KNX (USD 3,110,667) from T+14 to T+24, the peak of the schedule, about USD 37,300,000 a year at USD 0.16. Year two (T+13 to T+24) releases 231,300,000 KNX, USD 37,008,000 at USD 0.16 and USD 46,260,000 at USD 0.20.

Circulating supply moves from 5.30% at TGE to 11.70% at T+12, 13.44% at T+13 and 34.83% at T+24. Public unlock research finds that price impact typically begins about thirty days before a scheduled unlock, so the T+13 step is priced during T+12. The number a lead will ask for is monthly unlock value divided by 30-day spot volume; the sell-pressure model (item 2.4) computes it once a volume is entered and carries no volume assumption of its own. As arithmetic only, holding the T+13 unlock at 10% of turnover needs USD 27,906,667 of volume over 30 days, about USD 930,000 a day.

The largest line at T+13 is the new round, whose tokens are bought at USD 0.16 (main close) and USD 0.20 (top-up close), and the legacy release of 100,000,000 KNX is spread over four years at 2,083,333 KNX a month.

3. Emissions that fall on a clock

Mining and validator emissions are a pocket of 485,000,000 KNX (48.50% of supply) released under a hard annual cap of 48,000,000 KNX in year 1 that steps down 15% each year, with actual issuance the lesser of the cap and a usage-linked formula: KNX per verified attested task, halving annually. Modeled release is 4,000,000 KNX per month from T+4 to T+12 (36,000,000 in year 1), then 3,400,000 in year 2, 2,890,000 in year 3, 2,456,500 in year 4, 2,088,025 in year 5, 1,774,821 in year 6 and 1,508,598 in year 7: 205,415,332 KNX through T+84. The remaining 279,584,668 KNX of the pocket is a ceiling, not scheduled supply, and no token is minted beyond 1,000,000,000.

The remaining gap is enforcement: no pocket is hardcoded in a smart contract, and locking emissions, team and treasury on-chain before the listing submission is the next step.
