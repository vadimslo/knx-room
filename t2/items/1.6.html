1.6 Obligations and side terms

This register lists every obligation and side term that binds the company under a document executed by Konnex or signed by the counterparty. The two largest exposures are campaign agreements with TGE deadlines: the KOLpad Marketing Raise Agreement with Mindo Solutions Inc., as amended on 17 October 2026, fixes the TGE Date at 5 February 2027 with liquidated damages of USDT 75,000 for a later TGE and price protection for the KOL token allocations, and the Cookie DAO Snaps campaign agreement of 6 January 2026 fixed a TGE deadline of 15 May 2026 with a USD 200,000 fee on failure and USD 30,000 per week in tokens for any agreed extension. The remaining items are executive and advisor token terms, two confidentiality undertakings and the Foundation's General Power of Attorney.
