9.4 Dependency map: which subnets rely on which hardware partners

Tier 2 Filed, partial

In preparation. Subnet-to-hardware-partner dependency map.

The three live subnets each map to a named hardware partner: drone-navigation to ReadyMonitor's drone-in-a-box docks and to Exyn Technologies' autonomous aerial systems, roboarm VLA to Veloce's robot kitchen arms, and SLAM to Exyn's mapping robots. Exyn therefore sits behind two of the three live subnets, and drone-navigation is the only subnet with two partners. The other 27 subnets registered on the testnet have no partner recorded.
