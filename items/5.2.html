5.2 Pricing and take rate on stablecoin settlement, with unit economics by task type

Tier 1 Filed, partial

In preparation. Unit economics by task type.

Task settlement on Konnex is stablecoin-native: payers escrow stablecoins, executors are paid in stablecoins on verified completion, and the protocol takes a fee on the settlement flow that is swapped into KNX for the treasury. The fee parameters are under design. Three rates appear across Konnex documents, from 1 basis point to 50 basis points, and one rate will be fixed before mainnet.

Fee parameters

The protocol fee on stablecoin settlement is 50 bps (0.50%) of the executor payout, collected by the fee collector contract and swapped into KNX for the treasury. Third-party bond providers who post assurance stake on an executor's behalf receive a bond yield of 25 bps (0.25%) carved from the gross reward. Network gas is paid in KNX. These are the contract-level parameters of the stablecoin settlement design published at docs.konnex.world.

Illustrative splits in the whitepaper

The whitepaper contains one worked split, in the 2033 bridge-inspection scenario of §3.3: a lead robot wins a macro-contract and splits it into micro-tasks with a 60/35/5 fee split between lead, worker and AI provider. Payouts to executors are trust-weighted in stablecoins (§8.5: each executor's share is its trust score over the sum of trust scores, times the pool). These are design illustrations of how a task's value is divided among participants; they are not the protocol's take rate.
