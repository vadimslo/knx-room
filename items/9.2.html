9.2 Competitive analysis against peaq, BitRobot, OpenMind, PrismaX and GEODNET

Tier 1 Filed

None of the five peers verifies physical work with hardware-attested sensor evidence or settles robot labour in stablecoins on its own chain. peaq and OpenMind own the identity and operating-system layers and could add contracting on top; BitRobot and PrismaX sell training data; GEODNET is a supplier of positioning, not a competitor. Peer figures are as of 13 September 2026 with sources listed below; the Konnex column is on the Token Release Schedule of 17 September 2026 and the explorer snapshot of the same date.

peaq

Own Substrate L1 launched with its TGE in November 2024, repositioned by its 2026 "Purple Paper" as a chain-neutral coordination layer where fees are paid in ETH, SOL or USDC and PEAQ is used for staking, machine bonding and governance. About USD 44M raised in total; the token trades at USD 0.0247 with a USD 110M FDV, 97% below its December 2024 high, with 3.15B tokens still locked through 2035. It claims 3.3M machine identities and 200M transactions, but on-chain gas revenue is about USD 19k a year and activity is dominated by one application. The Robotics SDK (September 2025) covers identity, storage and access; a May 2026 demonstration paid Serve Robotics and LG robots in USDT on Solana with self-signed telemetry. It does not prove that work happened.

BitRobot Network

Built by FrodoBots Lab with Protocol Labs on Solana; subnets crowdsource embodied-AI data, teleoperation and compute for research buyers. USD 8M raised (USD 6M seed, February 2025, led by Protocol VC). No token: contributors earn non-transferable Bolts and no TGE date exists. A fleet of roughly one hundred to several hundred rovers, 50k teleoperation missions, 16k navigation hours, and a USD 5M Grand Challenges fund with DeepMind, Unitree and Toyota among partners. Verification is human and AI validators scoring output against subnet-defined metrics; cryptographic proofs are listed as future work. It sells data, not labour, and pays later.

OpenMind

OM1 is an MIT-licensed robot operating system running on nine hardware OEMs including Unitree, UBTech and Agibot, with a robot app store since February 2026; FABRIC is an identity and coordination layer on Base and Ethereum rather than a chain. USD 20M raised in August 2025 led by Pantera, plus a USD 2M IDO at USD 400M FDV. ROBO launched on 27 February 2026 and trades at a USD 93M to 133M FDV; settlement runs in USDC through Circle x402, so the token has weak value accrual. Proof of work and location are designed but not shipped; only the identity mint is live. A Sybil group captured about 40% of the airdrop with no clawback. OpenMind is the one peer that owns the robot-side stack and could add contracting by default.

PrismaX

Browser-based teleoperation of tabletop robot arms that produces training data; USD 11M seed in June 2025 led by a16z CSX. No token, no TGE, only points and a USD 99 membership. Supports eight robot models and reports about 500 participants; hours and customers are unverified. Its "Eval Engine" scores video with open-weight models and, since June 2026, community human validators. It has no sensor attestation, no on-chain contracts and no stablecoin payouts, and is outfunded by Lightwheel, Tacta and XDOF in its own data market.

GEODNET

The largest community-owned RTK GNSS network: 21,429 base stations in 170 countries, about USD 10M annualised data revenue in July 2026, 80% of which buys and burns GEOD; USD 208M FDV. It verifies only its own supply side (proof of location and accuracy of stations) and sells a correction stream to Quectel, DroneDeploy, Propeller and drone makers. For Konnex it is a positioning input to location attestation, not a competitor; the overlap is the "rails for physical AI" investor narrative.

Where Konnex stands

  • The only network in the set designed to combine a contract, a proof that the work happened and stablecoin settlement released by that proof. The nearest partial overlap is the Virtuals SeeSaw subnet on BitRobot (USDC escrow released after an evaluator check). On the testnet the proof runs on simulator evidence; the hardware attestation stack is specified in items 3.3 and 4.1.
  • The only one with enterprise workloads being integrated as subnets (ReadyMonitor, Exyn Technologies, Veloce).
  • The smallest public footprint of the four token projects: TGE target Q3 2027, testnet since April 2026, and the smallest fleet of physical robots on the network.
  • Token comparables: peaq at USD 110M FDV and OpenMind at about USD 100M FDV after launch, both well below their launch highs; GEODNET at USD 208M FDV is the only one with revenue-backed token demand, the model the KNX buyback design follows. The new round prices KNX at USD 160M FDV.

Threats to watch

  • OpenMind bundling FABRIC contracting into OM1 across nine OEMs: distribution Konnex does not have.
  • peaq adding task contracting on its stablecoin rails and its machine-identity base.
  • BitRobot launching a token and turning its research fleet and academic brand into a labour marketplace.
  • Any of the four announcing hardware-attested proof of work, which removes the main technical differentiator.
