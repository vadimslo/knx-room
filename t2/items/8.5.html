8.5 Key-person analysis and retention terms

Tier 2 Filed, partial

In preparation. Countersigned copies of the September 2026 executive agreements; retention terms for the CTO and CBDO.

Three people carry the operation. The President is engaged under a binding term agreement through TGE with compensation gated on a qualifying financing close. The Chief Technology Officer holds the protocol code and infrastructure and is one of the two signers on every token Safe. The Chief Business Development Officer is the other signer and holds the Foundation's power of attorney, the Protector appointment and the directorships of the operating entities. Retention terms are documented for the President and the Chairman; the Token Release Schedule records the remaining allocations (item 8.1).

In practice, any movement of tokens requires both signers, and any act of the Foundation or the operating entities passes through the Chief Business Development Officer. Loss of either signer would block token movements until the Safe configuration is changed, which itself requires the remaining signer.

Dependency on the Chief Technology Officer

The protocol is a Substrate L1 (subtensor fork) with subnets, an escrow settlement module and the evidence pipeline, all developed by the engineering organisation he leads. The token-contract repository and the protocol repositories are under his personal account pending assignment to the company (item 1.7); reviewer access to the private repositories is under NDA (item 3.8). Eleven of the sixteen filed FTE are engineers reporting to him (item 8.4). The documented terms for the Chief Technology Officer are the token allocation filed in July 2026.

Dependency on the President

Two of the three enterprise counterparties are his companies: Exyn Technologies, which he led until 25 August 2026, and ReadyMonitor, which he co-founded and whose MOU he signed as counterparty (items 1.5 and 5.1). His engagement runs only through TGE, and the Board holds the right to move him to another role. The agreement's public-description clause (§8.5) requires him to be described as Advisor until the appointment is announced.
