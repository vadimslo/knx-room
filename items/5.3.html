5.3 Hardware partner list and integration status against the plan

Tier 1 Filed

Five hardware counterparties appear in the documents: three enterprise counterparties whose robots are being integrated ahead of mainnet, and two drone companies at MOU-draft stage. On the testnet, three workload classes carry registered validators and miners at the 17 September 2026 snapshot: drone navigation, robot-arm vision-language-action control and SLAM 3D mapping. No hardware root-of-trust integration has been evidenced on a physical device yet; testnet evidence to date is simulator-generated (item 3.3).
