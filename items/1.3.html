Vesting terms

All legacy token holders, including the KOL round, vest on one schedule under Token Release Schedule: 0% at TGE, a 12-month cliff, then 48 months of linear monthly release from month 13 to month 60 after TGE. Release dates fall on the 15th of each month. TGE is target Q3 2027 (model working date 15 September 2027), so the first legacy investor release falls in October 2028. No legacy tranche unlocks at or within three months of TGE. Locked or unvested tokens cannot be staked or earn rewards.

The current round (200,000,000 KNX, 20.00%, at USD 0.16) vests on a shorter tail: 0% at TGE, a 12-month cliff, then 24 months linear from month 13 to month 36. Legacy holders therefore remain locked for two years beyond the last release to the current round.

Private-placement basis

  • Each round was a private placement to a small number of institutional and professional investors and sophisticated individuals; no public or retail distribution took place, no registered public offering was conducted and no registration statement was filed with any securities regulator.
  • The asset was not offered to US investors through these rounds.
  • Identity information was collected from each investor. Each investor and, for institutions, related parties were screened against the OFAC SDN and consolidated lists, the UN Security Council consolidated list, the EU consolidated financial sanctions list and the UK OFSI consolidated list, including comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions and Russia- and Belarus-related programmes; no investor returned a match. Screening practice is described in item 7.3.
