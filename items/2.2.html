2.2 Vesting, lockups and wallets

Every KNX pocket in the schedule has a stated release schedule and, except the new round and the KOLpad allocations, a Base wallet. No investor, team or emissions tokens are unlocked at TGE; the 53,000,000 KNX (5.30%) float is marketing obligations, the market-maker loan, the airdrop and the first KOLpad tranche. The full supply already sits in ten Gnosis Safes on Base, distributed in June and July 2026; moving those balances to the schedule targets and creating the new round wallet are pending.

Signers, thresholds and enforcement

The per-allocation multisigs are controlled by Vladimir Smelov (Chief Technology Officer) and Pablo Pérez Pazos (Chief Business Development Officer) as authorized signers. Signature thresholds are to be fixed before TGE. Vesting is to be administered by a third-party provider, not yet selected, with allocations custodied in the per-pocket Safes and released per the published schedule; no class is hardcoded in a smart contract. Distribution at TGE is on-chain from the per-allocation wallets, with the community airdrop released through a distribution platform and the KOLpad allocations placed on a transparent on-chain vesting platform before the first scheduled release.
