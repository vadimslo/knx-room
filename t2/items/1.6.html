1.6 Side letters, MFN and pro-rata obligations carried from prior rounds

Tier 2 Filed, partial

In preparation. Side letters, MFN or pro-rata terms attached to the seed or pre-seed, if any.

This register lists every obligation and side term that binds the company under a document executed by Konnex or signed by the counterparty. The largest exposure is the Cookie DAO Snaps campaign agreement of 6 January 2026, which fixed a TGE deadline of 15 May 2026 with a USD 200,000 fee on failure and USD 30,000 per week in tokens for any agreed extension. The remaining items are executive and advisor token terms, two confidentiality undertakings and the Foundation's General Power of Attorney.

Snaps Agreement (Cookie DAO campaign), effective 6 January 2026 Cookie DAO and Swift Growth Global Limited (BVI Company No. 2155275), acting as Konnex. Executed by both parties by electronic signature. Cayman Islands law. Cash fee of USDC or USDT 50,000 within seven days of the effective date. Token allocation to campaign participants of KNX worth USD 200,000 at a reference FDV of USD 200,000,000 (equivalent to 1,000,000 KNX, 0.10% of supply), distributed only through the Sign platform: 30% at TGE, a three-month cliff, then three months of vesting. FDV drop protection: if the allocation is worth less than USD 200,000 at the TGE unlock, additional tokens restore the value within three days. Tokens must be pre-funded to the Sign smart contract no later than one hour before TGE; each day of delay costs 10% of the allocation (USD 20,000) in USDC or USDT. Extension of the campaign services costs USD 15,000 per additional month. TGE deadline 15 May 2026, warranted by Konnex. If TGE does not occur by the deadline, a fee of USD 200,000 in USDT or USDC is payable within seven days. If the parties agree to extend the deadline, an additional fee of USD 30,000 for each seven-day period of extension, converted into tokens at FDV, is payable immediately after TGE. Campaign start no later than 15 January 2026, three-month duration.

No severance. Payment gate: first venture financing closing whose proceeds are received as unrestricted funds. Accrued amounts remain due after the end of the term.
