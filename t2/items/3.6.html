3.6 Slashing conditions and economic security parameters

Tier 2 Filed, partial

In preparation. Numeric slashing and stake parameters; only symbolic formulas exist.

Economic security rests on two stakes with different jobs: a KNX security stake, posted by validators and executors and slashed on protocol and consensus faults, and a stablecoin assurance stake, posted by validators on mainnet, that compensates counterparties for settlement-layer faults such as approving an invalid proof; executors and miners also lock stablecoin deposits per job that are forfeited on a failed or missing proof. Stake sizes scale inversely with the square root of on-chain trust, so untrusted actors post more. The formulas are published; the parameters that give them numbers are governance settings that have not been set.

The intended effect is collateral efficiency: a clean record raises T and lowers the required deposit, while a poor record raises it. The whitepaper's key metrics for the settlement layer include a dispute rate under 1 percent of jobs resolved without human intervention.

The reference settlement contract sketch on the stablecoins page carries two rate settings, a fee on the executor payout and a yield to third-party bond providers, with owner-settable caps; the whitepaper describes the fee only as small. The rate stated to a listing review in August 2026 for stablecoin settlement flows at launch was 0.01 percent (1 basis point), with a separate bridge withdrawal fee to be fixed at launch, and fees routed to a single on-chain protocol treasury contract.

What runs on the testnet

The testnet stakes testKNX only. Validators are stake-weight gated with KNX staking and weight aggregation follows the Yuma model inherited from subtensor. The audited runtime contains the konnex-escrow pallet: a payer funds an escrow naming its resolvers; a strict majority of resolvers releases; the payer refunds after the deadline; resolver votes after the deadline are rejected (added in remediation of finding N7); settled escrows are removed from storage (finding N8). Stablecoin escrow, the assurance tranche and BondMatrix are Phase 1 and Phase 2 items on the roadmap.
