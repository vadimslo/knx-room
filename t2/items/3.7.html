3.7 Third-party security audits, with remediation status

Tier 2 Filed

Two third-party audits exist. SlowMist audited the chain runtime's escrow pallet, coinbase and epoch code (2,264 nSLOC) on 23 and 24 April 2026, raised nine findings (one high, four medium, one low, three suggestions), and issued a Passed result with every finding fixed. QuillAudits audited the KNX ERC-20 token contract on Base on 20 July 2026 and found a single informational issue, acknowledged. Before mainnet the company has committed to a re-audit of the release candidate as a hard launch gate, separate audits of the KNX bridge and the stablecoin bridge before bridge activation, and a live bug bounty from mainnet.
