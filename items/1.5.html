Control concentration

The Foundation that owns the token issuer is controlled by one officer through the Protector appointment and the General Power of Attorney, and every allocation wallet is signed by two officers. The Chief Business Development Officer is therefore a related party to the issuer itself, not only to a counterparty. Decision rights and the intended multisig thresholds are set out in item 2.5; the governance documents are in item 1.8.

Customer concentration

Two of the three enterprise counterparties whose workloads are being integrated ahead of mainnet are connected to the President: Exyn Technologies, which he led until 25 August 2026, and ReadyMonitor, which he co-founded.
