A KOLpad campaign run by Mindo Solutions Inc. in June 2026 raised USDT 1,500,000 from KOLs against KNX priced at a 40% discount to the TGE valuation. All rounds were private placements to institutional and professional investors and sophisticated individuals, with identity and sanctions screening completed.

Vesting terms

All legacy token holders, including the KOL round, vest on one schedule under the Token Release Schedule: 0% at TGE, a 12-month cliff, then 48 months of linear monthly release from month 13 to month 60 after TGE, 2,083,333 KNX a month in total. Release dates fall on the 5th of each month. TGE is target Q1 2027 (model working date 5 February 2027), so the first legacy investor release falls in March 2028 (T+13, 5 March 2028). No legacy tranche unlocks at or within three months of TGE. Locked or unvested tokens cannot be staked or earn rewards.

The current round (195,000,000 KNX, 19.50%, for USD 32,000,000 in two closes: a main close of 175,000,000 KNX at USD 0.16 and a top-up close of 20,000,000 KNX at USD 0.20) vests on a shorter tail: 0% at TGE, a 12-month cliff, then 24 months linear from month 13 to month 36. Legacy holders therefore remain locked for two years beyond the last release to the current round.

The KOLpad allocations (15,000,000 KNX at the USD 0.20 reference, of which 12,500,000 KNX to the KOLs and 2,500,000 KNX to Mindo) release 20% at TGE, 40% one month after TGE and 40% two months after TGE. The count is fixed from the TGE valuation.

Private-placement basis

  • Each round was a private placement to a small number of institutional and professional investors and sophisticated individuals; no public or retail distribution took place, no registered public offering was conducted and no registration statement was filed with any securities regulator.
  • The asset was not offered to US investors through these rounds.
  • Identity information was collected from each investor. Each investor and, for institutions, related parties were screened against the OFAC SDN and consolidated lists, the UN Security Council consolidated list, the EU consolidated financial sanctions list and the UK OFSI consolidated list, including comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions and Russia- and Belarus-related programs; no investor returned a match. Screening practice is described in item 7.3.
