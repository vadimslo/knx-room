2.9 Listing status, venue commitments and confidentiality obligations

Tier 2 TOKEN Filed

KNX is under listing review at three venues on the basis of documents submitted between June and September 2026, and applications at further venues are at screening stage. No venue has committed to list, and the confidentiality terms accepted during these reviews prohibit representing any review as a listing.
