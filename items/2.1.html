2.1 Token Release Schedule

The canonical model is the Token Release Schedule dated 19 October 2026, filed in the room as a working Excel file rather than a picture of one. It carries 1,000,000,000 KNX across 18 pockets, a monthly unlock grid from the TGE working date to T+60, and the InvestorInsiders and Monetary Policy tabs that exchange templates require; a generated grid extends the schedule to T+84.

Generated unlock grid

The generated workbook 2.1_unlock-grid_r2.xlsx reproduces the schedule grid from parameters rather than pasted values and runs it to T+84. Inputs holds each pocket's total, TGE amount, linear start and end month and monthly amount, plus the emissions cap (48,000,000 KNX in year 1, 15% step-down). Grid computes the monthly unlock per pocket by formula for T+0 to T+84, with monthly total, circulating supply and percentage; Cumulative shows the per-pocket running totals. Check compares the formula output with the schedule circulating figures at T+0, T+12, T+24, T+36, T+48, T+60 and T+84 and every pocket total through T+84; all differences are below 1 KNX. Notes records the conventions of the grid: T+0 is the TGE working date with vesting on the 5th of each month, the KOLpad allocations release 20% at TGE and 40% at each of T+1 and T+2, and the team release runs T+14 to T+38 at 2,000,000 KNX per month with nothing at T+13.
