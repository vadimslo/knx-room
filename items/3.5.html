3.5 Mainnet roadmap with the subnets, dependencies and dates

Tier 1 Filed, partial

In preparation. Subnet list for mainnet with hardware dependencies per subnet; the phased plan with dates is filed.

The roadmap runs in three stages without calendar dates: the current public testnet (Substrate L1, faucet, three workload subnets with end-to-end task flow and on-chain PoPW records), Phase 1 mainnet economics and enterprise settlement (stablecoin escrow, dual stake, hardened registries), and Phase 2 scale-out (mesh transport, robot-to-robot contracts, bridges and ramps). TGE target Q3 2027 (model working date 15 September 2027). Mainnet launch is gated on audit sign-off of the release candidate, with no private mainnet phase.

Phased plan with dates

The operating plan of September 2026 sets four phases against the listing target of Q3 2027. Each phase is defined by what must be true before the next one starts, not by a calendar alone.

PhasePeriodGateContent
Phase 1January to June 2027A physical Proof-of-Physical-Work record on the public chain and paid third-party work against itTrusted-execution-environment signed capture on three device classes; 10,000 attested tasks across three customer sites in one regulated vertical; published dispute and false-positive rates with methodology; three to five paid pilots at USD 50,000 to USD 150,000 each; one insurer or broker quote priced against a Konnex record; a versioned Universal Task Language specification with two OEM implementations, or the claim withdrawn; sybil pass with published methodology before the airdrop snapshot; market maker engaged on a retainer six weeks before listing.
Phase 2Q3 2027Listing priced at or near the round priceToken generation event (model working date 15 September 2027); buy-and-burn of protocol revenue live from the first block, published monthly; stake-gated work routing for validators and fleet operators; venue sequencing against real volume; monthly scoreboard from month one.
Phase 32028 to 2029Burn exceeds emissions before the investor vest completesPilots to production contracts: base case 105 paying customers and 18,500 attesting robots across three verticals; each featured mainnet subnet launches only with a named paying counterparty; base case emissions at 0.7 times the burn in 2029; portable operating records and machine-cashflow financing against verified uptime.
