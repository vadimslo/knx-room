Tokenomics rationale

Every figure is from the schedule;

1. The float needs a cost basis

At TGE, 77,000,000 KNX is unlocked, worth USD 12,320,000 at the round price of USD 0.16, which is 38.50% of the USD 32,000,000 round. The float is marketing 40,000,000 KNX, liquidity 30,000,000 KNX, community airdrop 5,000,000 KNX, and 1,000,000 KNX each from treasury and Foundation. No investor, team or advisor token is in it; every investor sits behind a twelve-month cliff. That means nothing in the float was bought: 93.51% is company or Foundation inventory and 6.49% is a retroactive airdrop.

The design answers the first question a lead asks in two ways. Marketing, the largest single line in the float at 51.95%, is capped at 40,000,000 KNX at TGE and is to be itemised against actual listing and liquidity obligations, with the other 55,000,000 KNX of the pocket released over 30 months. Liquidity is sized at 30,000,000 KNX (3.00% of supply) as market-maker retainer inventory with terms to be published. What the design does not do is put purchased tokens into the float; the round's tokens begin to unlock at T+13.

2. The month-13 wall

Every investor cliff ends at T+13. In the schedule the T+13 unlock is 18,908,333 KNX, USD 3,025,333 at the round price: new round 8,333,333, emissions 3,400,000, legacy investors 2,083,333, marketing 1,833,333, treasury 1,633,333 and Foundation 1,625,000. The team release of 2,000,000 KNX per month begins at T+14, taking the monthly total to 20,908,333 KNX (USD 3,345,333) from T+14 to T+24, the peak of the schedule, about USD 40,000,000 a year at USD 0.16.

Circulating supply moves from 7.70% at TGE to 14.48% at T+12, 16.37% at T+13 and 39.37% at T+24. Public unlock research finds that price impact typically begins about thirty days before a scheduled unlock, so the T+13 step is priced during T+12. The number a lead will ask for is monthly unlock value divided by 30-day spot volume; the sell-pressure model (item 2.4) computes it once a volume is entered and carries no volume assumption of its own. As arithmetic only, holding the T+13 unlock at 10% of turnover needs USD 30,253,333 of volume over 30 days, about USD 1,008,000 a day.

The wall therefore changes character rather than disappearing: the largest line at T+13 is the new round, whose tokens were bought at USD 0.16, and the legacy release is cut to a fifth of its former size and spread over four years.

3. Emissions that fall on a clock

the schedule replaces it with a hard annual cap of 48,000,000 KNX in year 1 that steps down 15% each year, with actual issuance the lesser of the cap and a usage-linked formula: KNX per verified attested task, halving annually. Modelled release is 4,000,000 KNX per month from T+4 to T+12 (36,000,000 in year 1), then 3,400,000 in year 2, 2,890,000 in year 3, 2,456,500 in year 4, 2,088,025 in year 5, 1,774,821 in year 6 and 1,508,598 in year 7: 205,415,332 KNX through T+84. The remaining 224,584,668 KNX of the pool is a ceiling, not scheduled supply, and no token is minted beyond 1,000,000,000.

The remaining gap is enforcement: the schedule "hardcoded in smart contract" row reads "No" for every pocket, and locking emissions, team and treasury on-chain before the listing submission is the next step.
