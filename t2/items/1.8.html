1.8 Board consents, charter and current governance documents

Tier 2 Filed, partial

In preparation. Resolutions of the Foundation council and of the Dalithium Inc director authorising the creation of 1,000,000,000 KNX, the TGE and exchange applications.

Twelve constitutional and governance documents are filed for the three registered entities: the Foundation's charter, registers and appointments of 27 May 2026, Dalithium Inc's memorandum, registers and share certificate of 29 May and 2 June 2026, and Konnex Inc's articles of 29 January 2026. Together they show a Foundation run by a founder of record, a council of three, a Protector and an attorney-in-fact, a token issuer whose only director is the Foundation, and a separate Liberian company with a single director and officer.
