1.2 Entity map: DevCo, Foundation, token issuer, and which entity signs what

Tier 1 Filed

The token structure is a Panama private-interest foundation, Dalithium Foundation, that owns 100% of Dalithium Inc, the BVI company named as token issuer and development company. A third registered entity, Konnex Inc (Liberia), pre-dates the structure and is held personally by Pablo Pérez Pazos, Chief Business Development Officer. The three certificates of registration are filed with this item.

Who signs what

  • Exchange listing questionnaires and applications: Dalithium Inc is named as token issuer and development company, with Dalithium Foundation as its sole shareholder. Listing-review NDAs, consent forms and checklists of July 2026 were executed by Dalithium Foundation, signed by Pablo Pérez Pazos under the General Power of Attorney.
  • Foundation acts: council of three plus the Protector under the charter; day-to-day signing by Pablo Pérez Pazos as attorney-in-fact under the General Power of Attorney of 27 May 2026, which covers contracts, bank accounts, digital assets, wallets and keys of the Foundation, and expires on 27 May 2028 unless revoked earlier.
  • Dalithium Inc acts: the Foundation as sole corporate director; resolutions of shareholders by simple majority and of directors by majority under the Memorandum and Articles of 29 May 2026.
  • Token allocation wallets: ten Gnosis Safe multisigs on Base holding the 1,000,000,000 KNX supply, with Vladimir Smelov (Chief Executive Officer and Chief Technology Officer) and Pablo Pérez Pazos as authorised signers; thresholds to be fixed before TGE (target Q3 2027, model working date 15 September 2027).
  • Executive and advisor token agreements of 8 September 2026: signed for the company by Pablo Pérez Pazos as Director; the contracting party named in those agreements is Konnex Labs Ltd.
  • Konnex Inc: Pablo Pérez Pazos as sole director and officer.
