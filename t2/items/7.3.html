Section 7 · Legal and regulatory

7.3 KYC, sanctions and participation screening for miners and validators

Tier 2 TOKEN Filed, partial

In preparation. Written KYC, AML and sanctions policy covering investors, distributions, enterprise settlement and network participants.

Screening operates at four points, as described to listing venues: investors in the private rounds were identified and screened against the OFAC, UN, EU and UK sanctions lists with no match; the project's own distributions (token sales and the community airdrop claim portal) apply geo-IP controls, sanctions screening and on-chain wallet screening; enterprise settlement services are contract-based with KYC and AML at onboarding and IP geo-blocking as a secondary control; and the network itself is permissionless, with no protocol-level KYC for miners or validators and optional compliance gates per market or contract. This page states current practice as described to venues.

What is screened at the project's own distribution points

  • Geo-IP: access to the claim portal and any sale front-end is blocked from the restricted jurisdictions.
  • Sanctions: participants are screened against the OFAC, UN, EU and UK lists, and any person subject to applicable sanctions is excluded.
  • On-chain wallet screening: receiving wallets are screened for sanctions exposure and illicit-activity indicators before distribution.
  • Distribution mechanics: tokens are distributed on-chain from the per-allocation multisig wallets on Base per the vesting schedule through a third-party vesting and distribution provider, and the community airdrop through a distribution platform; unclaimed airdrop tokens remain claimable for 90 days from TGE and are then returned to the community reserve.

Regulatory status of the company

The company is not regulated or licensed in any jurisdiction, has filed no registration statement, and to its knowledge has never been the subject of a law-enforcement action, alleged market manipulation or regulatory investigation. It conducts no virtual-asset, electronic-financial or cross-border settlement business in or into Korea. The fiat on-ramp is a post-mainnet, limited-region beta with a payment processor to be named before it goes live.
