2.1 The live TRS model: the working file, not a screenshot

Tier 1 TOKEN Filed

The canonical model is Token Release Schedule, dated 17 September 2026, filed in the room as a working Excel file rather than a picture of one. It carries 1,000,000,000 KNX across 17 pockets, a monthly unlock grid from the TGE working date to T+84, and the InvestorInsiders and Monetary Policy tabs that exchange templates require.

Generated unlock grid

The generated workbook 2.1_unlock-grid_r1.xlsx reproduces the schedule grid from parameters rather than pasted values. Inputs holds each pocket's total, TGE amount, linear start and end month and monthly amount, plus the emissions cap (48,000,000 KNX in year 1, 15% step-down). Grid computes the monthly unlock per pocket by formula for T+0 to T+84, with monthly total, circulating supply and percentage; Cumulative shows the per-pocket running totals. Check compares the formula output with the the schedule circulating figures at T+0, T+12, T+24, T+36, T+48, T+60 and T+84 and every pocket total through T+84; all differences are below 1 KNX. Notes records two conventions taken from the schedule grid rather than its vesting text: the Foundation linear release runs T+13 to T+36 (text reads M12 to M36) and the Team release runs T+14 to T+38 at 2,000,000 KNX per month (text reads M13 to M38).
