Counterparty exposure on-chain

No KNX is loaned, pledged or held by a third party: the liquidity bucket is in a project Safe with no lending agreement, no market maker is engaged (item 2.8), and no vesting or custody provider holds tokens. Exposure to exchanges, custodians or market makers therefore stands at zero at the snapshot.
